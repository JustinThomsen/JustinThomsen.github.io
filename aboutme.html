Favorite Music Experiences

  • Foo Fighters at Wrigley Field

    A great night with my wife, meeting new friends, catching up with old friends, and listening to Dave Grohl and friends rock our faces off on the field at Wrigley!

  • Cake & Ben Folds Co-Headlining

    Two of my favorite bands playing together in one night. John McCrea even gave away a tree during the show!

  • Vandoliers at Codfish Hollow

    Watching anyone perform at the Codfish Hollow barn in Iowa is awesome. Pitch black star gazing out in the country at 3 a.m.

    Meeting John McCrea and blurting out "You're my hero!" taught me a great lesson about how celebrities are just normal people too.

    High-energy shows at Summerfest and Milwaukee proving that live music lovers can always rock out.
